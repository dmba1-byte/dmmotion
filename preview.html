Questions

Are the stories real?

The characters and plots are fiction, inspired by the history of accessible design. The numbers behind each one are real, from the 2010 ADA Standards.

Is the sheet really free?

Yes. Comment REACH on any reel or use the button above, and it's yours.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's a student's visual guide. Check the current standards and your local code for real projects.

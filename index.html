Before you ask



  
What file do you need?
Illustrator (.ai), SVG, a vector PDF, or DXF. From Revit or AutoCAD, export to DXF or PDF, or send the DWG and I'll convert it. A JPG or PNG of a drawing isn't enough for a plan; it is enough for a clean logo.

  
I only have a PDF. Does that work?
If it came out of Revit, AutoCAD, Illustrator or a plotter, yes. If it's a scan or a photo, send the original drawing file instead.

  
How long does it take?
Watermarked preview within five business days of the first payment. Clean file after two rounds of notes, usually within a week of the preview.

  
What if I want changes?
Two rounds of notes are included. Send all notes in one reply each round. Changing the drawing itself after the first preview counts as a new file.

  
How do I pay?
Venmo @David-Mba-1, Cash App $Dmba04, or PayPal. Half to start, half before the clean file.

  
Can I use the clip anywhere?
Yes. It's yours: website, listings, social, presentations, ads. Your drawing stays yours too; I don't reuse client files.

  
Does it have sound?
No. Clips are silent so you can add your own music or voiceover without a licensing problem.

  
Is it AI?
The animation is built by software from your file, directed and reviewed by me. Nothing is generated: no line in the clip is invented. Same file in, same clip out.

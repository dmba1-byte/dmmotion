IS THE GAME REALLY FREE?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download and no account.

WHERE DO THE NUMBERS COME FROM?

The 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design. The game shows the section number every time you pass.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Anyone 13 and up who is curious how buildings work: students, future architects, and designers who want a refresher.

IS THIS LEGAL ADVICE?

No. It is a learning tool. Check the current standards and your local code for real projects.

CAN KIT EXPLAIN SOMETHING FOR MY SCHOOL OR FIRM?

Yes. Custom Kit explainers are made to order. Email dmba04@gmail.com with the topic.

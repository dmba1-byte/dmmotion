Before you ask



  
What do you need for line weights?
A Rhino (.3dm) file with your 2D linework on layers (Make2D output, curves or layouts), the scale the drawing should be built to, and your line degrees for cut, profile, projection and hatch. No degrees? Ask for my default set. You get clean, consistent vector linework back to your scale and spec.

  
Is the line-weight service OK for school work?
The design, the thinking and the drawing stay yours; I do the technical line work to your specification. You are responsible for your school's rules on outside help. If your course restricts it on production work, don't order it for that submission. The order form asks you to confirm.

  
Do you keep or reuse my Rhino file?
No. I don't reuse or share client files. Your drawing stays yours.

  
What file do you need for animation?
Illustrator (.ai), SVG, a vector PDF, or DXF. From Revit or AutoCAD, export to DXF or PDF, or send the DWG and I'll convert it. A JPG or PNG of a drawing isn't enough for a plan; it is enough for a clean logo.

  
I only have a PDF. Does that work?
If it came out of Revit, AutoCAD, Illustrator or a plotter, yes. If it's a scan or a photo, send the original drawing file instead.

  
How long does it take?
Watermarked preview within five business days of the first payment. Clean file after two rounds of notes, usually within a week of the preview.

  
What if I want changes?
Two rounds of notes are included. Send all notes in one reply each round. Changing the drawing itself after the first preview counts as a new file.

  
How do I pay?
Venmo @David-Mba-1, Cash App $Dmba04, or PayPal. Half to start, half before the clean file. If your firm needs an invoice, say so and I'll send a PayPal invoice within a business day.

  
Can I get a refund?
Yes, before work starts: a full refund of the deposit if you change your mind within one business day, or if your file can't be used. After the watermarked preview, you can simply stop: the deposit covers the work done, you don't owe the balance, and the clean file isn't delivered. If the preview is late, you choose a full refund or $50 off. Once the clean file is delivered there are no refunds; that's what the two rounds of notes are for. Line weights: full refund before work starts, and corrections to match the degrees you specified are included. Prepaid plans: unused clips refunded at the per-clip rate.

  
Can I use the clip anywhere?
Yes. It's yours: website, listings, social, presentations, ads. Your drawing stays yours too; I don't reuse client files.

  
Does it have sound?
No. Clips are silent so you can add your own music or voiceover without a licensing problem.

  
Is it AI?
The animation is built by software from your file, directed and reviewed by me. Nothing is generated: no line in the clip is invented. Same file in, same clip out.

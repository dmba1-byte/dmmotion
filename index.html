Kit's Code Stories

Every story starts with a question you have probably wondered about, then shows the real rule and the person behind it. Under a minute each.

STORY 1

How high is the light switch in your house?

Ronald Mace used a wheelchair and spent his career on that question. He coined the term "universal design."

STORY 2

Why do hospital rooms feel designed against you?

An architect found out the hard way when he became a wheelchair user in 2003, and it changed how he designed.

STORY 3

Why does every street corner have a little ramp?

It started in 1940s Kalamazoo with one man asking his city to listen. Today the law gives the ramp a number.

Questions

Who is this for?

Anyone curious about how buildings work for everyone: middle and high school students, first-year architecture students, and designers who want a quick refresher.

Where do the numbers come from?

The 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design. Kit explains them in plain language and shows each one as a diagram.

Is the sheet really free?

Yes. Use the button at the top of the page and it is yours.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a student's visual guide. Check the current standards and your local code for real projects.

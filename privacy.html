Privacy Policy — Up To Code

Effective November 15, 2026 · Last updated November 15, 2026

Up To Code is a learning game about building accessibility and design codes, made by DM Motion. This policy explains what information the game does and does not handle. The short version: the game does not ask for your name, account, or any personal details, and your progress stays on your own device.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Up To Code has no sign-up, no account, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts, or location.

  • Game progress (which levels you have completed) is saved only in your device's local storage. It stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed if you clear the app's data.
  • Advertising. If the game shows ads, they are contextual, non-personalized ads. Ad providers may process limited technical data (such as your device type, general region, and an IP address) to deliver and measure those ads, under their own privacy policies. We do not use this data to build a profile of you, and we do not receive it in a form that identifies you.
  • No sale of data. We do not sell or share personal information, and we have none to sell.

Who the game is for

Up To Code is intended for people ages 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will address it.

How information is used

Because the game keeps your progress on your device and collects no personal information, there is very little to use. Any limited technical data handled by ad providers is used only to show and measure contextual ads and to keep the service working. We do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • You can clear all saved game progress at any time by clearing the app or browser data for Up To Code.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete, but you can still contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if advertising is added — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
